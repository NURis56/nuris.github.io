About

I'm Nurislam, a ninth-grader at Білім-Инновациялық Лицей (БИЛ) in Astana, about to start the IB Diploma Programme. I grew up in Shymkent and moved to Astana in 2024.

Most of my time goes into computer science — AI most of all — and competitive programming. Away from the screen I edit video: I cut our school film #nuris_absolute, and I volunteer with the Lumiere team.

I'm fluent in Russian and Kazakh, study in English, and I'm just starting Chinese.

The idea I keep coming back to: where you're born shouldn't decide your future. That's what Bagdar AI is about — students in any region should get the same guidance and chances as everyone else.
